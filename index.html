Working principles

Technology should make the work clearer—not harder to own.

Clarity

Understandable by the people who use it.

Plain language, visible decisions and documentation that supports independence.

Restraint

The right-sized solution for the real need.

No unnecessary features, tools or automation simply for the sake of complexity.

Responsibility

Privacy and human oversight from the start.

Sensitive information is treated carefully and important decisions remain accountable.

Durability

A foundation that can improve over time.

Maintainable systems with sensible structure instead of fragile shortcuts.
